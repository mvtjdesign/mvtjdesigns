Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026
Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

MVTJ Designs respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, shared and retained when you visit this website, contact MVTJ Designs about a project or choose to use a linked service.

This policy applies to the MVTJ Designs website. It does not automatically apply to independent websites or services linked from this site, which have their own privacy practices.

2. Information You Choose to Provide

This website does not currently provide an inquiry form. If you contact MVTJ Designs by email or WhatsApp, the information you choose to include may contain your name, contact details and project information or questions. If you schedule a call using Calendly, you provide the scheduling details requested by that service. MVTJ Designs does not control what fields those independent services request or how they process information.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

3. Information Collected Automatically

The website does not intentionally use advertising trackers or an analytics service. The hosting provider that delivers and protects the website may process ordinary technical request information, such as an IP address, browser or device details, requested pages and resources, referring page and approximate request time, in server or security logs.

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts. The Cloudwards page also requests a video thumbnail from YouTube; its video player is loaded from YouTube when you choose to play it. Those providers may receive technical information needed to deliver the requested resource.

4. How Information Is Used

Information that you choose to provide may be used to respond to your questions, understand a proposed project, discuss services or estimates, and arrange a call you request. Technical information may be used to deliver and secure the site, diagnose issues, prevent abuse, keep appropriate business records, meet legal obligations and protect MVTJ Designs’ rights.

This website does not offer an email marketing sign-up.

5. Legal Bases, Where Applicable

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, information may be processed to take steps you request before a contract or to perform a contract; based on legitimate interests in operating and protecting the business and responding to inquiries; with your consent when it is specifically requested; or to comply with legal obligations. Which basis applies depends on the circumstances.

6. How Information May Be Shared

MVTJ Designs does not sell personal information for money. Information may be shared only as reasonably necessary with:

  • Providers that host, deliver or protect the website;
  • Google when your browser requests Google Fonts;
  • Calendly when you choose to schedule a call, and WhatsApp when you choose to contact MVTJ Designs through that service;
  • YouTube when your browser requests the Cloudwards video thumbnail or you choose to play the embedded video;
  • Professional advisers when needed;
  • Government or legal authorities when disclosure is legally required, or other parties when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety or website security; and
  • A successor in a business restructuring or transfer, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Independent services have their own privacy policies and practices. MVTJ Designs does not control them.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website code reviewed for this policy does not set first-party analytics or advertising cookies, and no analytics or advertising tracker is intentionally installed. The site does not currently show a cookie-consent banner. Hosting infrastructure or third-party resources and services may use cookies or similar technologies under their own practices, particularly when you visit or interact with those services. The YouTube player is requested only after you activate the video, although its thumbnail is requested when the Cloudwards page loads.

8. Data Retention

Information received in a business inquiry is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, evaluate a project, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements or satisfy legal obligations. Any retention by independent services is governed by their own policies.

9. Data Security

MVTJ Designs uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for information it handles. No method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access to, correction or deletion of personal information; object to or request restriction of certain processing; withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; request portability where applicable; or complain to an appropriate regulator.

To make a request, email maxtijerino@gmail.com. MVTJ Designs may need to reasonably verify your identity before responding. MVTJ Designs will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights that apply to you.

11. Children’s Privacy

This website and its services are intended for adults seeking professional services and are not directed to children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes a child has submitted personal information to MVTJ Designs, please contact us using the details below.

12. Third-Party Links

This website links to independent portfolio, social, scheduling, messaging and media services. When you follow a link or use an embedded service, that provider’s privacy policy and terms apply. Please review those policies before sharing information with them.

13. International Visitors

MVTJ Designs is based in Cyprus and works internationally. Depending on the service involved, information may be processed in Cyprus or another country where MVTJ Designs or a service provider operates. Privacy protections may differ between jurisdictions.

14. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as the website or business practices change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when material changes are made.
