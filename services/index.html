Starting Scope

The package includes one discovery questionnaire, one 15-minute project call, and one responsive page with up to five structured content sections, optimized for desktop, tablet and mobile. Launch is on the client’s existing domain and compatible hosting.

Typical Delivery

7–10 business days after the initial payment and all required content, assets, approvals, and access have been received.

Payment

50% to begin and 50% before launch.

Copy

The client supplies the initial written content and business information. MVTJ Designs refines and organizes the supplied content for clarity and presentation. Full copywriting from a blank page is quoted separately.

Design

The page is styled to complement the client’s existing logo, colors, typography, and approved brand assets. Complete brand identity development and multiple unrelated design directions are not included at the starting price.

Revisions

One revision round means one consolidated list of reasonable amendments submitted after the initial page presentation. Additional revision rounds or a new creative direction are quoted separately.

Form and Integrations

The starting scope includes one standard inquiry form. CRM automation, payment processing, conditional workflows, booking-system configuration, and custom API integrations are quoted separately.

Analytics

Advanced analytics, dashboards, advertising management, reporting, and ongoing conversion optimization are not included at the starting price. Any approved tracking implementation must be clearly defined in the proposal.

Post-launch Support

Seven days of support covers defects in the approved delivered page. New content, new functionality, third-party service issues, and ongoing updates are not included.

What We Need from You
