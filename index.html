Our Process

From Opportunity To Approved Asset

01

Opportunity

We identify the client questions, founder insights, daily activity, and business moments worth turning into content.

02

Capture

Your team captures voice notes, photos, office moments, event clips, founder thoughts, and raw context.

03

Options

The system turns raw material into strategic content options: posts, scripts, graphics, FAQs, carousels, and brand assets.

04

Approve & Ship

You approve or request changes. MVTJ prepares, publishes, tracks performance, and uses the results to guide the next move.
