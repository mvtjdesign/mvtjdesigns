View full scope details

Copy

The client supplies the initial written content and business information. MVTJ Designs refines and organizes that material for clarity, hierarchy, and presentation. Market research, messaging strategy, and writing all content from a blank page are not included.

Design

The page is styled to complement the client’s existing logo, colors, typography, imagery, and visual direction. Complete brand identity development and multiple unrelated design directions are not included.

Page Scope

The package includes one landing page containing up to seven structured content sections. Additional pages, duplicated campaign versions, language versions, and substantial new sections are quoted separately.

Revisions

One revision round means one consolidated list of reasonable amendments submitted after the first complete page presentation. Additional revision rounds, a new creative direction, or changes outside the approved scope are quoted separately.

Form

The package includes one standard inquiry form. CRM automation, payment processing, conditional workflows, membership functionality, booking-system configuration, and custom API integrations are quoted separately.

Analytics

Basic analytics and inquiry tracking means connecting an existing supported analytics account and measuring the page’s primary inquiry action when the client supplies the required access. Dashboards, advertising management, monthly reporting, and ongoing optimization are not included.

Launch

Launch support covers publishing the approved landing page to the client’s existing domain and compatible hosting environment. Domain registration, hosting charges, platform subscriptions, migrations, and unusual infrastructure work are not included.

Post-launch Support

Seven days of post-launch support covers defects in the approved delivered page. New content, new functionality, third-party service problems, and ongoing website updates are not included.

What We Need from You

  • Approved logo and existing brand assets
  • Initial written content and accurate business information
  • Approved images and media
  • Domain and hosting access
  • Analytics or form-service access where required
  • One primary decision-maker
  • One consolidated revision list

The delivery period begins after the initial payment and all required content, assets, and access have been received.
