01 / Project Overview

Creating More Than a Website

Create + Elevate began with a request for a simple three-page static website. The opportunity was broader: establish the identity, positioning and digital presence of a new property visualisation and marketing company serving developers, architects and estate agents.

MVTJ Designs developed the project into a cohesive platform designed to explain the company’s services, present its visual capabilities and guide prospective clients toward the appropriate next step.

View complete homepage
02 / The Challenge

Turning a New Business Into a Credible Market Presence

This was a new digital presence. The work needed to bring identity, presentation and enquiry together from the outset.

  • A recognisable visual identity
  • Clear differentiation between visual-production and marketing services
  • Premium positioning appropriate for property professionals
  • A way to demonstrate capabilities before a substantial client portfolio existed
  • Structured paths for enquiries and introductory consultations
  • A responsive experience suitable for international visitors
03 / The Strategic Idea

CREATE the Assets. ELEVATE the Development.

Two clear service categories transformed a long service list into a more understandable commercial proposition.

CREATE

Architectural visualisation and presentation assets.

  • Exterior and interior imagery
  • Aerial and site-context views
  • Animation
  • Floor plans and virtual tours

ELEVATE

Commercial and marketing support.

  • Brochures and launch content
  • Landing pages and campaigns
  • Lead generation
  • Agent sales materials
04 / Visual Identity

A Quiet, Architectural Identity

The studio’s visual direction balances architectural imagery with a restrained editorial composition.

  • Refined serif typography
  • Black, warm ivory and muted gold
  • Restrained editorial composition
  • Architectural imagery as the primary visual language
  • A horizontal wordmark and compact CE monogram
  • Premium presentation without visual excess
