The Challenge

Cloudwards needed a repeatable production system that made complex technology topics accessible while preserving accuracy, clarity and audience trust at scale.

The Process

I designed and managed the workflow shown above, combining content strategy, production management and asset organization with research, scripting, on-camera presentation, editing, publishing and distribution. Standardizing each stage supported consistent delivery and editorial quality.

The Outcome

  • Supported scalable, presenter-led educational media through research, hosting, post-production, asset management, publishing and delivery.
  • Maintained consistency across production, file organization and delivery.
  • Helped explain cloud storage, VPNs, cybersecurity, streaming access, password management and digital privacy to a global audience.
  • Demonstrated production discipline, media operations and on-camera brand presentation.
